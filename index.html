📋 Quick Teacher Setup Guide

  1. Open your Google Sheet on your computer ➔ click Extensions ➔ Apps Script.
  2. Click Deploy ➔ New deployment ➔ Web app (or Manage deployments ➔ Edit ➔ New version).
  3. Ensure Who has access is set to Anyone, then click Deploy and copy the URL.
  4. Paste the URL into Section 1 on your laptop, then click 📱 Send URL to Tablet (QR / Link) to configure your tablet without typing!
