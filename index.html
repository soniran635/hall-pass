📋 Quick Teacher Setup Guide

Follow these steps to connect your sheet in 2 minutes:

  1. Open your Google Sheet on your computer $ ightarrow$ click Extensions ➔ Apps Script.
  2. Click Deploy ➔ New deployment $ ightarrow$ Select type Web app.
